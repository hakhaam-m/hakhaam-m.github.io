Objective

Open to junior art positions in game and animation studios, and to undergraduate programmes in Animation or Game Art, with scholarship support. Self-taught artist with a production portfolio across pixel art, digital painting, vector illustration and 3D, and developer of an open-source Blender pipeline add-on.

Education

  • Diploma in Photographics — برهان Vocational School, Tehran. An interdisciplinary programme combining image-making, photography, print and digital output.
  • Self-directed study (following the diploma): pixel art, digital painting, vector illustration, 3D modeling and Python tooling for Blender — learned project by project, documented in the portfolio.

Technical Skills

  • Expert: Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Aseprite
  • Advanced: Blender (modeling + Python scripting)
  • Intermediate: Autodesk Maya, Adobe After Effects
  • Working knowledge: Fusion, ZBrush
  • Development: Python (Blender API), colour pipelines (OKLab), sprite/normal-map baking, Godot 4 resource export

Selected Project — ChiaroPixel (open source, GPL-3.0)

  • Blender add-on (4.2–5.2 LTS) that bakes 3D characters and props into 2D pixel-art sprite sheets with camera-space normal maps, specular and emission masks, JSON atlases and Godot 4 resources.
  • Perceptual OKLab palette quantisation, ordered Bayer dithering locked across animation frames, EV-calibrated three-point lighting drawn from studio photography practice.
  • Multi-direction (1/2/4/8) turnaround baking with automatic dynamic-relighting verification renders.

Artistic Practice

  • Pixel art: game environments, characters with walk/attack animation frames, and asset sets (weapons, shields, vehicles) at production sizes (32×32 and below).
  • Digital painting: environmental concept art, character portraits and weapon/prop assets.
  • Vector: polygonal animal illustration and minimal cartoon character design.
  • 3D: hard-surface product modeling (e.g. headphones, broadcast camera, aircraft) with studio lighting.

Statement

I chose self-education over an immediate university place and built my skills through daily, project-based practice. That path taught me discipline and tooling depth; formal study is the next step — I want the fundamentals, mentorship and collaborative production environment of an Animation / Game Art degree, and to bring a toolmaker's mindset to it.
